How to play?

1.
To play, you need to enter the bet amount and choose the color on which you bet. You can choose two different colors at the same time.
2.
Red and black win gives you 2x payout. Green one - 14x payout!
3.
If you choose the correct color you will win 2x or 14x your bet amount! However, if you choose the wrong color you lose your bet.
