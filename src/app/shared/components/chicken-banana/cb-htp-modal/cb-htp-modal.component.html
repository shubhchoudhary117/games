How to play?

1.
Place your bet.
Select the amount you want to bet before each round begins (0.01 - 150 EUR).
2.
Start the game.
Press the PLAY button to launch the round. Five random symbols will appear and fix in the upper field.
3.
Reveal cards.
A 5x4 grid of closed cards will appear. You can open cards manually by tapping them or use the GO button to reveal a random card automatically.
4.
Symbol types.
Different symbols are hidden on the field: Low, Medium, and High. Look for special Jackpot symbols: Mini (x25), Major (x100), and Mega (x1000).
5.
Win conditions.
Find 3 matching symbols to win. Once the match is found, the round ends, and all remaining cards reveal automatically.
6.
Bonus Game and Free Spins.
Match 3 chest symbols to launch the Bonus Game with the most valuable rewards. Three FS symbols activate between 1 and 10 free rounds.
7.
Guaranteed win.
Losing is impossible in Chicken-Banana. Every round is guaranteed to end with a 3-symbol match and a payout.
8.
Auto Game.
Hold down the PLAY or GO button to activate automatic mode. Select the number of auto-spins (from 10 to 1000), and the game will continue until they are completed.
9.
Technical malfunctions void all payouts and games.
