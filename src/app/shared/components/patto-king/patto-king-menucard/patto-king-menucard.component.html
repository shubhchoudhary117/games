How to Play

1
💰 Set Your Coins
Use the − / + buttons in the footer to choose your bet amount. Minimum 10 coins, maximum 500 coins per deal.
2
🃏 Deal Cards
Press the DEAL CARDS button. You will play 5 rounds at once — each round deals 3 cards from a freshly shuffled deck.
3
👀 Watch the Cards
Cards flip one by one. If two or three cards share the same rank, they will be highlighted with a green glow.
4
🔥 Teen Patti — Jackpot!
If all three cards share the same rank — e.g. K♠ K♥ K♦ — you hit Teen Patti and win 5× your bet for that round!
5
⚡ Do Patti — Double Win!
If any two cards share the same rank — e.g. A♠ A♥ 7♦ — you hit Do Patti and win 2× your bet.
6
📊 Add Up Your Total
All winnings from Teen Patti and Do Patti across 5 rounds are added together and credited to your coins. Multiple wins in a single deal are possible!
⚡ Quick Reference
🔥 Teen Patti (3 of a kind) Bet × 5
⚡ Do Patti (2 of a kind) Bet × 2
✗ No Match 0
💡 Pro Tip: Each deal has 5 rounds — win more rounds, earn more! Teen Patti is rare but pays the most.
